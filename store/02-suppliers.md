# Supplier Shortlist — AU/NZ Fulfilment

The rule: **if it ships from China to an NZ customer, you are selling a 2–3 week delivery.** That destroys conversion rate, generates refund requests, and under NZ/AU consumer law an inaccurate delivery estimate is itself a breach (see `03-compliance-au-nz.md`). Local supply costs more per unit and is still the cheaper option once you count refunds, chargebacks and ad spend wasted on people who abandon at the shipping estimate.

---

## Tier 1 — Start here (AU/NZ warehouses)

### Dropshipzone
- **AU/NZ-focused**, 100,000+ products (tools, fitness, furniture, kids' apparel, homewares)
- Most supply partners warehouse in Australia, shipped by local carriers
- **2–5 business days to metro addresses**
- Native Shopify integration
- **Best for:** bulkier/homeware/tools products where China shipping is impossible anyway. This is the most AU/NZ-native option on the list.

### Syncee
- Multi-supplier marketplace with **verified NZ suppliers**, auto order sync, Shopify app
- Acts as a control tower across several suppliers rather than being one supplier
- **Best for:** finding a local supplier in a specific category once you've named the niche.

### CJdropshipping (Australian warehouse only)
- Huge catalogue, **has AU warehouses** — AU-warehoused items reach customers in days vs 7–17 days from China
- Sourcing requests: you can ask them to source a product and stock it
- **Critical:** filter to the AU warehouse. The default catalogue is China-shipped and is not viable for this store.
- **Best for:** trending/impulse products where you want CJ's catalogue breadth.

### Gelato (NZ, print-on-demand)
- **NZ-based production**, ~3-day delivery
- **Best for:** if the niche has an apparel/print/accessory component, or for branded merch as a margin add-on later.

## Tier 2 — Useful, with caveats

| Supplier | Note |
|---|---|
| **Spocket** | US/EU/AU supplier network, 3–6 day delivery in-region, better product quality and review rates. Margins run **30–40%** — lower than China-sourced, because local costs more. Check AU/NZ supplier depth in your category before subscribing. |
| **Zendrop** | Beginner-friendly, polished UI, good support, US-focused fulfilment. Less AU/NZ relevant — evaluate only if it has AU stock in your niche. |
| **AutoDS** | Not a supplier — an automation layer across multiple suppliers. Worth it at volume, not at launch. |
| **Local NZ/AU wholesalers direct** | The highest-margin option nobody bothers with: email 10 NZ or AU wholesalers in your niche and ask about dropship or consignment terms. No app fee, better pricing, real relationship. Slow to set up, worth it. |

## Non-negotiable supplier checks before you list a product

1. **Order a sample to your own address.** Time it. Photograph the unboxing. That sample is also your first batch of ad creative — budget NZ$50–150 for samples, it is the single best-spent money in the launch.
2. **Confirm the warehouse location in writing**, per SKU. "AU warehouse" on a listing is not the same as "this SKU is in the AU warehouse today."
3. **Ask what happens on a faulty item.** Under AU/NZ law it's your problem, not the supplier's — but a supplier who replaces free is worth a higher unit cost.
4. **Check stock depth.** A supplier who runs out mid-campaign while you're paying for ads is an expensive lesson.
5. **Check it isn't Meta/TikTok ad-restricted.** Anything health-adjacent, weight-related, or making a body claim will get accounts disabled. Check before, not after.
