## Down, bedding & regulation (added for the duvet build)

### Enforcement — down content claims
- [Quilt suppliers stop false and misleading '100%' down content claims — ACCC](https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/quilt-suppliers-stop-false-and-misleading-100-down-content-claims) — sample testing found **none** of the suppliers' products achieved claimed 100% down; actual content 50–90%. Downia quilts claiming 100% tested at 80–90%; a David Jones quilt claiming 100% goose down tested at 88%
- [Australian Feather Mills Pty Ltd — s.87B undertaking — ACCC](https://www.accc.gov.au/public-registers/undertakings-registers/section-87b-undertakings-register/australian-feather-mills-pty-ltd-s87b-undertaking) — undertaking to make no 100% down claim, and no down-percentage claim unless substantiated by **testing on finished products**. The AS 2479 tolerance defence did not prevent enforcement
- [Harris Scarfe Australia — s.87B undertaking (PDF) — ACCC](https://www.accc.gov.au/system/files/public-registers/undertaking/778656-1-Undertaking.pdf)
- [ACCC acts on Conroys "Product of Australia" misrepresentation](https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/accc-acts-on-conroys-%E2%80%9CProduct-of-Australia%E2%80%9D-misrepresentation) — country-of-origin exposure
- [Australia greenwashing: ACCC/ASIC prosecutions review](https://natlawreview.com/article/australia-greenwashing-half-yearly-review-asic-and-accc-prosecutions-and-accc) — greenwashing as a current enforcement priority

### Biosecurity — importing down into NZ
- [Importing fibre and feathers — MPI](https://www.mpi.govt.nz/import/fibre-skins-trophies/fibre-feathers) — animal fibre and feathers must meet biosecurity requirements to enter NZ
- [Steps to importing fibre and feathers — MPI](https://www.mpi.govt.nz/import/fibre-skins-trophies/fibre-feathers/steps-to-importing) — may require a veterinary certificate confirming the fibre is disease-free or processed in a specified way; requirements set by the applicable Import Health Standard
- [All import health standards — MPI](https://www.mpi.govt.nz/legal/compliance-requirements-secondary-legislation/import-health-standards-ihs)

### Animal welfare certification
- [Responsible Down Standard — Textile Exchange](https://textileexchange.org/responsible-down-standard/) — prohibits live-plucking and force-feeding; every supply-chain stage independently audited including unannounced visits; **products may only be called RDS Certified if they contain 100% RDS-certified material with chain-of-custody documentation** (there is no partial RDS). Still the definitive active down standard in 2026 — down was explicitly excluded from Textile Exchange's Materials Matter Standard (Dec 2025)
- [RDS — Control Union](https://germany.controlunion.com/en/certification-program/rds-responsible-down-standard/)
