# Product Scorecard

Score every candidate out of 100. **Below 70, don't build the store.** Nothing in this business is as expensive as committing to the wrong product and then trying to fix it with better ads.

| # | Criterion | Points | How to check |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | **AU or NZ warehouse available** | 15 | Confirm per-SKU in writing with the supplier. No local stock = score 0 here and reconsider entirely. |
| 2 | **3x+ markup at a NZ$60+ price point** | 15 | `python3 tools/unit_economics.py`. Must print "[OK] Viable". |
| 3 | **Demonstrable in a 3-second video** | 15 | Can someone see the benefit happen, with no voiceover? Splash, snap, before/after, transformation. If it needs explaining, organic won't work. |
| 4 | **Solves a specific, annoying problem** | 10 | Name the person and the moment of annoyance in one sentence. If you can't, it's a commodity. |
| 5 | **Not ad-restricted** | 10 | Check Meta + TikTok policy. Health, weight, body-attribute, medical = risk of a disabled account. |
| 6 | **Repeat purchase or clear add-on** | 10 | Consumables and refills beat one-off purchases. Repeat buyers are where the actual profit is, and they cost $0 CPA. |
| 7 | **Not sold in every AU/NZ supermarket** | 5 | If Kmart has it for $12, you're not competing. |
| 8 | **Ships small, light, non-fragile, no battery/liquid** | 5 | Lithium batteries and liquids attract courier surcharges and air-freight restrictions. |
| 9 | **Real demand evidence** | 10 | Google Trends AU+NZ trending up 12 months; TikTok hashtag view counts; existing sellers with engaged comments (competition is proof of demand, not a reason to avoid). |
| 10 | **You can stand behind it under CGA/ACL** | 5 | You're the seller of record. Would you defend this product to a customer, a regulator, and your own family? |

---

## Scoring guide

| Score | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 85+ | Build it. |
| 70–84 | Build it, but fix the weakest criterion first. |
| 55–69 | Keep looking. This is the score that bankrupts people — good enough to start, not good enough to win. |
| Under 55 | No. |

## Demand research, free, in one afternoon

- **Google Trends** — set region to New Zealand *and* Australia separately, 12-month view. Flat or declining = no.
- **TikTok search** — the product term, filter to recent. Are creators posting about it, and are the comments "where do I get this" (buying intent) or just likes (entertainment)?
- **AliExpress / Amazon AU order counts and reviews** — read the 3-star reviews specifically. That's where you find the flaw your listing needs to pre-empt, and often the product improvement that differentiates you.
- **Existing AU/NZ sellers** — if 2–3 local stores sell it and have been running ads for months (check Meta Ad Library, it's free and public), the product converts. If nobody does, ask why before assuming you found a gap.
- **Reddit / Facebook groups** in the niche — search for the complaint the product solves, in the customers' own words. Those words become your ad copy.
