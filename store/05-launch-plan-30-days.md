# 30-Day Launch Plan — NZ$500–2,000 Budget

Written for the budget and traffic mix chosen: AU/NZ market, organic short-form first, paid Meta/TikTok second.

## Budget allocation (NZ$1,000 mid-case)

| Line | Amount | When |
|---|---:|---|
| Product samples (2–3 candidates) | $150 | Week 1 — **spend this first** |
| Domain | $30 | Week 2 |
| Shopify Basic × 3 months | $120 | Week 2 (take the free trial first; the clock doesn't start until you have a product) |
| Apps | $0 | Month one runs on free apps and the free Dawn theme |
| Reserve for samples of a second product | $150 | Week 4, if product one fails |
| **Paid ad test** | **$550** | Week 4 onward, and not a dollar before |

At the $500 end: samples $120, domain $30, Shopify $80 (annual/trial), ads $270. At the $2,000 end, the extra goes to ads and a UGC creator, not to apps or a theme.

**The rule the budget enforces:** no money to Meta until an organic video has proven the hook. You are buying amplification, not discovery.

## Week 1 — Validate before you build (spend: $150)

| Day | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Name the niche. Run 5–8 product candidates through `06-product-scorecard.md`. Keep only 70+. |
| 2 | Run every survivor through `tools/unit_economics.py`. Kill anything that doesn't print "[OK] Viable". |
| 3 | Contact 3–5 suppliers (`02-suppliers.md`). Ask: AU/NZ warehouse per SKU? Real dispatch and delivery times? Faulty-item process? Stock depth? |
| 4 | **Order samples of your top 2.** Start a stopwatch on delivery — that measured time becomes your shipping policy. |
| 5 | Demand research (`06-product-scorecard.md` §research). Meta Ad Library: who else is running this in AU/NZ, and for how long? |
| 6 | Open the TikTok/IG account for the brand. Start posting *before* the store exists — niche content, no product. Zero cost, and the algorithm needs the runway. |
| 7 | Decide: product one. Written down, committed. |

## Week 2 — Build (spend: $150)

| Day | Do |
|---|---|
| 8 | Samples arrive → **film everything**. Unboxing, the product working, the problem it solves, close-ups. One session gives you 30 days of content. |
| 9 | Buy domain. Start Shopify trial. Dawn theme. No apps. |
| 10 | Product page: your own photos, your own copy. Never the supplier's — theirs is on 500 other stores and often makes claims illegal in AU/NZ. |
| 11 | Policy pages from `03-compliance-au-nz.md` §4. Real delivery times from your Day 8 stopwatch. |
| 12 | Shopify Payments setup. Test a live order end-to-end with your own card, then refund it. |
| 13 | Meta pixel + TikTok pixel installed and firing. Verify in the events manager — a pixel installed on week 4 has no data to optimise on. |
| 14 | **Post organic video #1.** Store live. |

## Week 3 — Organic signal (spend: $0)

- **3–5 short videos per day.** Same offer, different hook and different first frame. This is the highest-leverage fortnight in the whole plan.
- Every video needs an **event in the first second** — something moves, opens, spills, transforms. A static product shot is a dead video. (This is the clearest finding from the prior project's research: the same caption on a static clip got 323 views; on a high-energy clip, 46,364.)
- Track per video: views, saves, comments-with-intent ("link?", "where"), and clicks to site.
- **Reply to every comment.** Comment volume is the cheapest ranking signal there is.
- Target by day 21: **one video over 10,000 views**, and your first organic sale.

## Week 4 — Paid amplification (spend: $270–550+)

- **Only run ads using creative that already performed organically.** If nothing hit 10k views, do not start ads — go back to week 3 with new hooks. This decision saves more money than any other in the plan.
- Start: **$20–30/day**, one campaign, Advantage+ or a broad ABO, 3 creatives.
- Judge on: CPA vs your calculator's max CPA, and break-even ROAS. Nothing else.
- Kill a creative at 2x your max CPA with no sale. Scale a winner 20%/day, not 200%.
- End of week 4: either the product clears break-even ROAS → scale, or it doesn't → you've spent <$1,000 total, kept the store, and move to product two with everything else already built.

---

## What "working" looks like at day 30

Realistic, not promotional: **3–15 orders, roughly break-even, and one piece of creative that reliably gets views.** That last item is the actual asset — profit follows a repeatable hook, not the other way round.

## What kills stores in the first 30 days

1. Buying a theme and 6 apps before making a sale.
2. Running ads before having an organic winner.
3. A China-shipped product with a 3-week delivery on a page that promised 5 days.
4. Sub-3x markup — no amount of ad optimisation fixes the arithmetic.
5. Quitting at day 21, which is roughly when the organic account starts working.
