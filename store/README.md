# AU/NZ Shopify Dropshipping Store — Build Repo

**Status:** foundation built, niche pending.
**Market:** Australia + New Zealand. **Budget:** NZ$500–2,000 for first 90 days. **Traffic:** organic short-form + paid Meta/TikTok.

## Read in this order

| File | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `01-market-brief-au-nz.md` | The 2026 conditions this business has to survive. Read once, don't skip — one rule change here decides your supplier. |
| `02-suppliers.md` | Who can actually ship to an AU/NZ customer in under a week, and what it costs. |
| `03-compliance-au-nz.md` | GST thresholds, consumer law, and the three things the ACCC/ComCom actually fine people for. |
| `04-unit-economics.md` | The maths that decides if a product can work **before** you spend on ads. |
| `05-launch-plan-30-days.md` | Day-by-day, with the budget split. |
| `06-product-scorecard.md` | Score any product idea out of 100 before committing. |
| `tools/unit_economics.py` | Runnable calculator. `python3 tools/unit_economics.py --help` |

## The one-line strategy

Sell an AU/NZ-warehoused product to AU/NZ customers, at a 3x+ markup, validated with free short-form video before any ad money is spent. Local supply is not a nicety in 2026 — it's what makes the numbers and the consumer law work.

## What's NOT in this repo yet

- The niche/product (you're naming it)
- Brand name, domain, logo
- Shopify store itself (nothing gets paid for until a product passes `06-product-scorecard.md`)
