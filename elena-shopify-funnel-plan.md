# Elena + Shopify: what to sell, what it costs, what will get it shut down
Written 21 Sep 2026. Builds on the page audit (17 Sep) and the OLIVE DM setup guide.

---

## 1. Read this part first

Elena is a synthetic persona making first-person claims — "I'm 67", "my skin didn't fade", "here's what actually worked for me". As an Amazon affiliate that is risky. As the owner of a Shopify store selling your own product, **you become the advertiser**, and the exposure changes completely.

The FTC's final rule on fake reviews and testimonials took effect 21 Oct 2024. It expanded the definition of "endorser" to cover anyone who *could appear to be* a person — which explicitly sweeps in virtual influencers and AI-generated personas. In January 2026 the FTC issued its first warning letters under the rule to ten companies. Civil penalties run up to **$53,088 per violation**.

Two changes make the difference between a business and a liability:

**1. Disclose the persona.** Bio line plus an on-reel tag. Where the endorser is synthetic, the FTC's 2026 framework treats the AI disclosure as separate from and additional to the sponsorship disclosure — one does not cover the other. The audit already found the comparable: `@morganmeanswell` tags `#syntheticperformer` and sits at 39.2K followers. Disclosure did not cost that account its reach.

**2. Change what Elena claims.** There is a bright line here and it decides the product:

| Elena as… | Example | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Testimonial** | "I'm 67 and this supplement gave me my energy back" | Fabricated personal experience with a product you sell. This is the thing the rule bans. |
| **Author** | "Ten Mediterranean habits, one page each, with the reason each one works" | A guide written in a persona's voice. Sold as a product, disclosed as AI. Defensible. |

So: **sell what Elena knows, not what Elena's body supposedly proves.** That single distinction rules out the supplement as your own product and rules in the guide.

## 2. What to sell

**Don't sell supplements under your own brand.** AI persona + health claims + your own label is the worst combination available: FTC endorsement exposure, health-claim exposure, Meta ad rejections, plus inventory and liability. Keep NAD+ as an affiliate link if you want, where the manufacturer carries the product claims.

**Don't start with physical goods** (olive oil, silk pillowcases). Inventory, shipping, returns and customer service on an account with 68 followers is solving a problem you don't have.

**Start with one digital product.** Zero cost of goods, instant delivery, no returns, and it's the product Elena is credible selling — she's a voice and a method, not a body. Digital is the highest-margin category on Shopify precisely because there's no unit cost.

The structure, which fits the OLIVE funnel already written:

| Tier | What | Price | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | "Elena's 10 Nonna Habits" one-pager | £0 | The OLIVE lead magnet. Gets the DM opened and the follow. |
| Paid | "The Nonna Method" — 30-day Mediterranean skin & energy reset: daily routine, recipes, shopping list, the reason each habit works | **$19–27** | The actual product. |

A note on affiliate links inside a paid PDF: Amazon restricts Special Links in downloadable and offline materials. Point the guide at a page on your own site that carries the links instead of embedding `amzn.to` in the file.

## 3. What it costs, and why not to open Shopify yet

**Shopify Basic (2026):** $39/month billed monthly, $29/month billed annually. Shopify Payments takes 2.9% + 30¢ per online transaction with no added Shopify fee; use a third-party gateway instead and Basic adds 2% on top. Digital files need a delivery app (Shopify's own free Digital Downloads, or SendOwl / Fileflare / Sky Pilot). ManyChat's Shopify integration — the thing that makes DM-to-cart work — sits on the Pro tier at $29–39/month.

Realistic monthly fixed cost before a single sale: **$70–80.**

**The no-monthly-fee alternatives:**

| Platform | Fixed | Per sale | Net on a $19 sale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Payhip** free plan | $0 | 5% + processing | ~$17.20 |
| **Gumroad** | $0 | 10% flat | ~$17.10 |
| **Stan Store** | $29/mo | 0% platform fee | $19 minus processing |
| **Shopify Basic** | $39/mo + apps | 2.9% + 30¢ | $18.15 |

Shopify's per-sale economics genuinely are better — about **$0.95 more per $19 sale** than Payhip. But $0.95 against $39/month means Shopify only wins past **roughly 40 sales a month**, and past **~70 sales a month** once ManyChat Pro is in. That's $800–1,400/month in revenue.

Current state of the account: 68 followers, ~650 average plays on the seven non-viral reels, single-digit keyword comments. Walk the funnel at a generous 3,000-play reel — 1% comment the keyword is 30 comments, 80% get the DM delivered (only if follow-gated), 5–10% buy — and that's **1 to 3 sales a month**. Shopify at $78/month against $40 of revenue loses money every month it's open.

**Recommendation: open on Payhip's free plan, not Shopify.** Same product, same DM funnel, same link, $0 fixed cost, and nothing to unwind. Move to Shopify the month you clear ~40 sales — the migration is a file upload and a link change, roughly an hour.

If you'd rather be on Shopify from day one regardless — it's a reasonable call if you're committed and want one platform to learn — then take the **annual plan at $29/month**, use **Shopify Payments** so you avoid the extra 2%, use the **free Digital Downloads app**, and **skip ManyChat entirely at first**. A DM with a plain product link converts fine; you don't need cart integration until there are carts. That's $29/month all in.

## 4. The funnel, end to end

1. Reel, disclosed as AI, with an event in the first second (audit section 4) and a withheld list.
2. Caption: "Comment OLIVE and I'll send the full 10. Follow first or Instagram buries it 🫒 #ad #syntheticperformer"
3. Comment triggers the automated DM — no link (see the DM setup guide).
4. They reply → 24-hour window opens → free one-pager delivered.
5. End of the free one-pager: "The full 30 days is here" → store link.
6. 24 hours later, one more message: one habit, no ask.

Comment-to-DM converts 3–5x better than manual replies, purely on speed of response. Instagram traffic also abandons carts at ~91%, the highest of any source, which is the honest argument for the ManyChat/Shopify cart recovery later — but only once carts exist.

---

## 5. Open questions before anything gets built

1. **Which country are you invoicing from?** Decides Shopify Payments eligibility, and whether you're registering for GST/VAT on digital goods. Digital products are taxed where the buyer is in most jurisdictions.
2. **Is the ice-plunge clip yours?** Still unanswered from the 17 Sep audit, and it's now more than a reach question — a store attached to reposted footage is a bigger target than an affiliate link was.
3. **Price: $19 or $27?** $19 for volume and a lower decision threshold; $27 if the guide runs to real depth (recipes, a shopping list, 30 dated days).
